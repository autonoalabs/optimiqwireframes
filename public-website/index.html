A little more clarity

Before you begin.

Which consultant role should I choose?

Start with your main area of interest: Consultant, Strategic Advisor or Project Manager. The portal uses a shared CTQ form to assess fit across all three roles, and you can select multiple suitable roles after submission.

Can a consulting firm also source consultants?

Yes. A firm can offer its services and request consulting expertise for its projects. The relevant role is handled within each project under the agreed engagement process.

Does the planner predict my earnings?

No. It divides your own annual gross billing goal by your daily fee assumption. It does not predict project availability, certification, fees, expenses or take-home income.

Do I need to register again if I already have an account?

No. Use member sign in with the account you created during registration.
