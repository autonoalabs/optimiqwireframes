Release plan for approval

One platform, delivered in two phases

Phase 1 launches the shared member portal through CTQ for Consultant, Consulting Firm, and Client. Phase 2 extends the same platform into qualification, opportunities, tendering, project delivery, administration, and integrations. The Phase 2 groups below are a proposed design and delivery sequence; they are not separate applications or committed dates.

Release boundaries

The public CTA forms already exist. They appear in the review journey for context and are not new development screens.

Phase 1 · Member onboarding and CTQ

One shared identity and sign-in; the approved Theme 1 shell and mobile navigation across three audience flows.

ConsultantExisting CTA → sign-in → terms → eight-section CTQ → role-fit selection.
Consulting FirmExisting CTA → sign-in → terms → six-section CTQ.
ClientExisting CTA → sign-in → terms → optional three-question Client CTQ. Its exact placement remains open.
Platform foundationAccount routing, role-aware access, CTQ data and versioning, draft/submit, private scoring and admin-controlled settings needed for this release.

Dashboards and every later member journey screen stay in the review inventory. The groups below are proposed implementation increments.

2B · Demand and matchingRequirements, Seller/Buyer/Win-and-Staff, masked matching, shortlists and bid preparation.
2C · Tender to controlled awardTOR, evaluations, decision, agreements, award and introduction.

Phase 2 screen placeholders

These are design destinations for client review. Their business rules remain subject to the decisions shown below.

2A · Qualification

Assess and qualify

Show progress and evidence without exposing internal score weights.

  • Consultant profile and composite assessment
  • Firm qualification and empanelment
  • Client dashboard with internal categorisation kept private
Clarify component rules, score treatment, certification bodies, firm bands, and client classification mapping before detailed assessment screens.
2B · Opportunities

Source and match

Keep Consultant, Firm Seller/Buyer/Win-and-Staff, and Client tracks distinct within one platform.

  • Consultant opportunities
  • Firm Seller / Buyer / Win-and-Staff
  • Client requirement and masked shortlist
Clarify search access, shortlist visibility, matching criteria, bid and reservation rules.
2C · Tender and award

Evaluate and contract

Model a controlled process from requirement and TOR to decision and signed agreements.

  • Track B tender and evaluation
  • Firm bids and proposals
  • Agreements and award
Clarify TOR fields, technical/financial parameters and QCBS rules, evaluator rights, LOI order and agreement signers. The agreed signature gate remains before award and introduction.
2D · Delivery and operations

Run and learn from projects

Track each engagement separately, including concurrent Consultant assignments and project-specific feedback.

  • Consultant engagements and performance
  • Client delivery, feedback and documents
  • Staff administration and controlled changes
Clarify feedback score effect, billing rules, project access, document reuse/consent, and channel/provider choices.

Decisions needed before detailed screen design

These questions affect workflow, data or visibility. The linked concept screens remain placeholders until the relevant decision is confirmed.

1 · Client classification and CTQ location

How do Type A/B/C and Strategic/Priority/Standard map to internal CL1–CL4 and Track A/B? Does the optional Client CTQ appear after CTA or after portal sign-in and terms?

2 · Consultant and Firm evaluation

Which composite components, weights, evidence and third-party results apply to each audience? Is the committee interview scored or an exception review? What does the Firm 0–3 / 4–7 / >7 branch count?

3 · Tender and award governance

What TOR fields, technical/financial parameters, QCBS formula, decision rights and disclosure rules apply? Which agreements and signers precede award, and where does the LOI sit?

4 · Performance and commercial flows

Does project feedback change the composite score or stay separate? What are the billing, fee, refund and Consultant/Firm payment rules?

5 · Communications and integrations

Which portal messaging, email, WhatsApp, chatbot, AI calling, meeting/calendar and notification capabilities are required, in which release, and with what consent and provider?

6 · Staff operations and knowledge

Which OptimIQ teams need which screens and rights? What documents can each party access, and what consent is needed before a project becomes a reusable case study or success story?
