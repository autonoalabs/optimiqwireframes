The previous three Why points covered only certification. The material omissions were the local–global model, flexible engagement/delivery choices, scoping and senior coordination, consultant opportunity and firm growth. The new six-item summary brings these together using the existing three-column component, stacked on mobile. No CSS, navigation structure, fonts or base layout are changed.

Existing approved handover wording supports Solution Coordinator scoping and optional Project Success Management; neither is a universally included or free service. "Optional senior delivery oversight" preserves that qualification. Project/retainer/success-fee arrangements describe choices agreed in advance, not fixed commercial policies.

## Source cautions retained

- The observed Services link returns "This Page Does Not Exist"; it provides no additional verified feature detail.
- The old site uses absolute claims about premium work, savings, unlimited scale and friction-free delivery. The summary avoids guarantees, fixed savings and automatic access.
- Independent institutional recognition is a long-term goal on Certified, not an accreditation already obtained. The firm page's institute co-signed credential is not promoted as an established fact here; the existing certifying-organisation clarification remains unresolved.
- The old firm FAQ itself leaves individual practitioner listings unconfirmed. It does not override the approved one-firm-login architecture. Tier names, staff accounts and scoring rules are unchanged.
- Homepage benefits are a concise public explanation, not a new approval of eligibility, rates, certification stages, external credentialing or business terms.

## Published copy

The right expertise. A better way to work together.

**Evidence before introduction** — OptimIQ Certified builds trust through proven work, professional judgment and ongoing review.

**Local insight, global expertise** — Combine GCC and Saudi understanding with international specialists, without a fully international team’s overhead.

**Support from scoping to delivery** — Match your challenge to consultants, advisors and project managers, with scoping support and optional senior delivery oversight.

**Flexible work. Clear terms.** — Agree scope and fees upfront: project, retainer or success-fee arrangements, delivered on-site, remotely or through a hybrid team.

**Recognition and relevant opportunities** — Consultants build standing on delivered work and connect with projects that fit their expertise.

**Firm growth, on your terms** — Access selected mandates and specialist capacity while keeping your firm’s brand and client relationships.
